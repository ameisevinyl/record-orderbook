# Pricelist and quote (v1)

Prices live in `src/pricelist.json`, not in `config.js`. Pure lib, no UI yet.

- **Items** are derived from CONFIG for every enabled format: records (`black`/`colour`/`random` from `vinylColor`), the products of inner sleeve / cover / inlay, reference cut, testpress, and, for every format, the mastering items `<format>/mastering/{lacquerCut,plating1,plating2}` (not in config: they belong to every release; flat prices per side) and `<format>/record/setup` (the pressing machine, once per order). Packaging extras such as plastic bags wait for a packaging category. Keys are format-scoped (`7/outerCover/cover-printed`); labels are no item, they are part of the record price.
- **Price**: net, per `unit` pieces (1000, or `"order"` = flat), in `tiers` from a quantity upwards, with an optional `min` line total. `null` = not filled in, `0` = included. `vat` (`ES`, 21) is stored for later and not applied; quote stays net.
- **Local vs template**: `src/pricelist.json` is gitignored and wins; `src/pricelist.example.json` (sample numbers) is the committed fallback, like `plant.config.local(.example).js`.
- **Tooling**: `build/pricelist.js` `extract` / `generate` (adds new items, keeps prices, drops removed ones) / `check`. `.claude/agents/pricelist-filler.md` maps a plant's own price list onto the keys; it never invents a price.
- **Editor**: `dist/pricelist.html`, a second single-file page (build.js `PRICELIST_FILES`), same design tokens as the order form. Open a `pricelist.json` (button or drop), edit in a dense table and save it back as a download. Rows are items; columns are `fixed` (flat price per order) and the quantity breaks (derived from the tiers; `+` adds a column, `x` removes it and its prices, the header quantity is editable). Prices are per piece. A price in `fixed` makes the item flat, one in a quantity column makes it per piece (the other kind goes). Also min total and VAT. Starts from the embedded template. It edits prices only; the item list stays `generate`'s job.
- **Plant config in the editor**: the same page opens/edits/saves `plant.config.local.js` (imprint address, transfer; a second Open/Save pair, download only, comments replaced by a short header; build embeds the config the order form is built with as the starting point). The pricelist's `vat.country` and `vat.rate` are derived from `imprint.countryCode` and a standard-rate table (`src/lib/vat-rates.js`, EU-27, GB, CH) and copied into `pricelist.json`, which stays self-contained; an unknown country keeps the rate editable and warns. After saving the config: copy it to `src/plant.config.local.js` and rebuild.
- **Quote** (`src/lib/quote.js`): tier = highest `from` reached (below the first: the first); line = `max(min, qty / unit × price)`; sleeve, cover and inlay count the whole pressed quantity; mastering is on top: lacquer cut and plating (`1step`, or `2step` father & mother — a lib option, not in any frontend yet) × sides (1 or 2), then the pressing setup once, hidden from customers once a UI exists; colours of one class share a tier. Unpriced items are returned in `missing`, never as 0.
- **Not yet**: VAT logic, discounts (customer/season), UI (customer page / plant view), quote in the order documents, repress pricing.
